01 / About

Curious by
default.

The best analysis begins with a better question. I bring an editorial eye to research and a human pulse to the numbers.

Not a futurist. Not a fortune teller. A careful observer with a healthy suspicion of easy answers.

My practice combines cultural research, qualitative interviews, social listening, and visual analysis. The output is always designed to travel: a clear narrative, a sharp set of implications, and a way for a team to act on what it has learned.

I am especially interested in the small shifts that become big changes: the new rituals, coded language, and unexpected communities that tell us where people are headed before the mainstream catches up.
